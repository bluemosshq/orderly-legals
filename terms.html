3. How Orders and Payments Work

  1. A Seller creates an order specifying the item(s), price, shipping fee, and buyer details.
  2. Orderly generates a secure checkout link, which the Seller shares with the Buyer.
  3. The Buyer pays for the order through the checkout link via our payment processing partner, Paystack.
  4. Funds are held in escrow and are not released to the Seller immediately.
  5. The Seller marks the order as shipped and provides an estimated time of arrival ("ETA").
  6. Once the Buyer confirms receipt of the order, or the order is automatically released as described in Section 5, funds are credited to the Seller's Orderly wallet, less any applicable fees.
