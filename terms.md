## 3. How Orders and Payments Work

1. A Seller creates an order specifying the item(s), price, shipping fee, and buyer details.
2. Orderly generates a secure checkout link, which the Seller shares with the Buyer.
3. The Buyer pays for the order through the checkout link via our payment processing partner, Paystack.
4. Funds are held in escrow and are not released to the Seller immediately.
5. The Seller marks the order as shipped and provides an estimated time of arrival ("ETA").
6. Once the Buyer confirms receipt of the order, or the order is automatically released as described in Section 5, funds are credited to the Seller's Orderly wallet, less any applicable fees.

## 5. Escrow, Delivery Confirmation, and Auto-Release

Funds paid by a Buyer are held in escrow until one of the following occurs:

- **Buyer Confirmation**: The Buyer confirms that the order was received in satisfactory condition. Funds are released to the Seller immediately.
- **Automatic Release**: If the Buyer does not confirm receipt within 48 hours of the Seller's stated ETA, Orderly will automatically release the funds to the Seller. This automatic release is intended to protect Sellers from indefinite fund holds and is a condition of using the Service that both Sellers and Buyers accept by using Orderly.

Buyers are strongly encouraged to inspect their order promptly upon delivery and confirm or report an issue before the automatic release window elapses.
